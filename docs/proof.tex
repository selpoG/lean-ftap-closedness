\begin{abstract}
We prove Theorem~4.2 for bounded real-valued semimartingales.
Starting from NFLVR for the terminal market of general stochastic integrals,
we obtain Fatou closedness and weak-star closedness. The proof constructs
auxiliary decompositions for the selected gains, component estimates with
common convex weights, and realization of the limit as a general integral
against the original price. The assumptions and proof boundaries correspond
to those used by the public Lean theorem.
\end{abstract}
